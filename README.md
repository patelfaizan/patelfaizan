## 📌 Featured Projects

### 1. Integrated FEA–Machine Learning Framework for Fatigue Life Prediction

**Domain:** Mechanical Engineering | FEA | Fracture Mechanics | Machine Learning

A research-oriented framework that integrates finite element analysis with Machine Learning to investigate fatigue crack growth and predict fatigue life in engineering materials.

**Key objectives**
- Develop a computational workflow for fatigue crack growth analysis.
- Study stress intensity factors and crack-length-dependent behavior.
- Apply Paris' Law to estimate fatigue crack growth.
- Develop a structured dataset combining simulation parameters and fatigue-related variables.
- Train and evaluate Machine Learning regression models.
- Investigate prediction accuracy and the relationship between simulation inputs and fatigue life.

**Methodology**

1. Develop the Compact Tension (CT) specimen geometry.
2. Define material properties, loading conditions, boundary conditions, and mesh.
3. Perform FEA and extract relevant fracture mechanics parameters.
4. Evaluate stress intensity factor ranges and fatigue crack growth rate.
5. Apply Paris' Law and numerical integration to estimate fatigue life.
6. Prepare data for Machine Learning.
7. Train and evaluate regression models.
8. Compare predictions against appropriate analytical, numerical, or experimental reference data.

**Technologies:** ANSYS Workbench, Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, Matplotlib.

**Engineering significance:** This work explores how numerical simulation and data-driven prediction can complement one another in fatigue assessment and engineering design.
